# Elemental balance: options (2026-10-05, nothing implemented)

Problem (from play): Lightning, Explosive, Kinetic and Pierce clear the map and dominate every build. Vortex, Poison and Ice are rarely worth packing. The 2026-10 gateway work already gave Poison (mines, turrets) and Explosion (area) real roles. Vortex and Ice are still the weak ones. Heat is on hold and infuser loops are fine for now, so these options do not depend on either.

## What makes each weak element weak today
- **Ice**: slows enemy projectiles it touches and chills targets, but a dead enemy is a better "slow". Value only appears against fast or high-HP targets, and it costs damage share.
- **Vortex**: pulls things, which scatters the fight and drags the player too unless an Anchor is packed. It adds no damage of its own.
- **Poison**: damage over time arrives after the target has already died to a stronger element.

## Options (can be combined)
1. **Make crowd control a multiplier, not a side effect.** Frozen, paralysed and vortex-held targets take bonus damage from other elements (for example +25% from Kinetic/Explosive while frozen). One pack slot of Ice then raises the whole build's output. Cheap, fits the existing status system, and rewards mixed builds. Risk: Ice becomes mandatory, so cap the bonus and make it expire.
2. **Give each weak element a payoff the strong ones cannot copy.** Ice: shatter, where a frozen enemy killed by Kinetic or Explosive bursts into shards. Vortex: collapse, where enemies held for 1 s take an explosion-scaled implosion hit. Poison: spreading, where poisoned kills pass the status to neighbours. These are discovery moments (matches "discovery is the puzzle") and need no number nerfs.
3. **Raise the cost of the dominant elements instead.** A per-element energy tax or cooldown on Lightning and Explosive. This is the Heat idea in a lighter form; it is on hold, so only do it if option 1 and 2 fall short.
4. **Enemy counters.** Armour or resistances that make Kinetic/Pierce weaker against some roles (heavy brawlers shrug off Kinetic, shielded casters shrug off Lightning) and make Ice/Poison/Vortex the answer to specific enemies. The enemy AI already breeds counters, so this also feeds the gene pool.
5. **Mythic and rarity hooks.** Tie the rarest tiles to the weak elements (a Mythic Catalyst for Vortex that removes self-pull), so chasing them is a build goal.

## Recommendation
Do 1 and 2 together first. They only add upside to the weak elements, are testable headlessly with the existing victim rig, and do not touch the strong elements. Re-measure pack rates afterwards from run logs before considering 3 or 4.
